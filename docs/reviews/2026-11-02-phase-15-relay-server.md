# Review 2026-11-02 – Phase 15, relay server and `RelayClient`

Review by Codex (`codex exec -s read-only`) over `apps/relay`,
`packages/relay/src/client.ts`, `protocol.ts` and `docker/relay`. Codex
reproduced the findings with Node scripts against the real source (files in
memory, no network). Every correction began with a red test in
`apps/relay/src/relay.test.ts`, `client.test.ts` or `options.test.ts`.

| Severity | Location    | Finding                                                                                                                          | Check                | Consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| high     | `relay.ts`  | An oversized frame without login triggers an `error` event without a listener and ends the process.                              | confirmed (red test) | `error` listener per connection.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| high     | `protocol`  | The proof is not bound to the relay: a malicious relay passes the challenge of another relay through and logs in there.          | confirmed (red test) | `signedChallenge(nonce, origin)`; the client signs its configured address, the relay checks against `HANGAR_RELAY_URL` (mandatory outside loopback).                                                                               |
| high     | `client.ts` | Eight hex characters of the fingerprint in the name of the remote bay: a second, suitably searched key inherits the permissions. | confirmed (red test) | Whole fingerprint in `remoteBayId`.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| high     | `client.ts` | Incoming calls without a limit; after the caller's timeout the handlers keep running and pile up.                                | confirmed (red test) | At most 10 running answers per instance, counted until the handler ends.                                                                                                                                                           |
| high     | `relay.ts`  | A receiver that does not read makes the relay buffer without end.                                                                | confirmed (red test) | Above 8 MiB unsent the connection is cut; the sender learns `offline`.                                                                                                                                                             |
| high     | `client.ts` | Messages from the relay without a size limit (Node WebSocket has none).                                                          | confirmed (red test) | Client on `ws` with `maxPayload` 2 MiB and a permanent `error` listener.                                                                                                                                                           |
| medium   | `relay.ts`  | A revoked member redeems an invitation secured earlier and becomes a member at the relay again.                                  | confirmed (red test) | `join` rejects revoked members (`Revoked`).                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| medium   | `client.ts` | If another member signed the current list, a valid invitation fails; the invitation is used up.                                  | confirmed (red test) | Codex's way (accept the list of any member) **not adopted**: the relay could forge a list with a foreign signer. Instead `join` waits (`'waiting'`) for the next list of the confirmed inviter, which is created on adding anyway. |
| medium   | `client.ts` | `connect` hangs on a close before `ready` or a silent relay; requests without an answer hang forever.                            | confirmed (red test) | `close` before `ready` fails, time limit for handshake and requests; sending on a closed connection fails immediately.                                                                                                             |
| medium   | `client.ts` | Same function over two workspaces: if one path drops, it disappears entirely.                                                    | confirmed (red test) | Offers remembered per path; another path of the same instance takes over (`takeOver`).                                                                                                                                             |
| medium   | `relay.ts`  | Invitations without a limit; each one grows `relay.json`.                                                                        | confirmed (red test) | At most 20 open invitations per workspace.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| medium   | `relay.ts`  | `relay.json` without `fsync`: after a crash a used-up invitation or a revocation can come back.                                  | confirmed (code)     | `writeDurable` (extracted from `ReplayGuard`). Not testable without a real power failure.                                                                                                                                          |

Own finding along the way: the option `confirmed` of `RelayClient` was read
nowhere; removed (the confirmed inviter comes via `join`).
