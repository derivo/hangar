## Overall assessment (Codex, abridged)

The architecture holds for the current scope. Bus, MCP, scheduler and SDK
have separate responsibilities; Electron stays at the desktop edge (apart from
`host.ts`, `index.ts`, `prompter.ts`, `updates.ts` only the preloads). The hubs
of the graph – `SessionManager`, `Host`, `Scheduler`, `Bus` – are the critical
coupling points; the findings mostly concern their lifecycle and state
transitions. Git calls use argument lists, the CLI shim quotes, bay IPC is
assigned via the sender. Most important open correction: a dedicated execution
channel for jobs.
